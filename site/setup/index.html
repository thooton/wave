    Preparing the setup form…

    The download may contain live credentials in plain text. Don’t share it or sync it to a shared drive. Delete your computer’s copy after the import.

    Import on the phone

    1. Open the app once to find its setup screen. Use a version that supports setup-file import.
    2. Connect the phone in File transfer mode and copy the download to Download or .
    3. In the app’s setup screen, choose the file and confirm the import. If the app couldn’t delete the source file, remove it from shared storage yourself.

    This version uses files, not QR codes.
