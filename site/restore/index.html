03 / STOCK RECOVERY

Verify your stock boot image.

Check a backup or a readback here. This page does not connect to the phone or write anything to it; to write the stock image back, use Stock boot.

Local backup check

These files stay in your browser. Use the original untouched 32 MiB image from this phone. A valid header or matching hash cannot prove the file came from the right phone: check the serial and firmware in your original backup notes.

Full XP3800 boot partition read before any changes.

JavaScript and a secure context are required to check SHA-256.





Get the phone into emergency download mode


If the phone boots but a Magisk module broke something, try Rescue first. If Android will not boot, the XP3800 needs a cold start for EDL:


  1. Unplug USB. Remove the back cover and battery and wait about 30 seconds.
  2. Hold Volume Up and Volume Down together. Keep holding as you refit the battery and plug in USB.
  3. Release after a few seconds and check the USB ID. Use only 05c6:9008. If you see 05c6:900e, that is crash-dump mode, not EDL. Unplug, remove the battery again and repeat the cold start.


See the USB and driver guide for your computer.


Restore and verify


Stock boot writes your saved image over EDL and requires all 32 MiB to read back identical before restarting. It accepts only a full image with the XP3800’s stock signature that matches its manifest or recorded SHA-256. Restoring stock boot does not uninstall Magisk or Wave, clear their data, or undo other changes; uninstall unwanted apps through Android Settings.


If you cannot identify the correct stock image, stop. Do not try a boot image from another phone.
